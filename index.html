NAS/FTP Photo & Video ViewerTorchPhoto

Uploading is easy, but viewing again is often not.
TorchPhoto is designed to help you browse photos and videos stored on NAS and FTP servers like a gallery on iPhone and iPad.

Download on the App Store

Features

Simple Workflow

From Server Setup to Gallery Browsing

Register your FTP server, generate thumbnails, and start exploring your media in a smoother gallery-style flow.

Step 1

Add Your FTP Server

Connect your server once and keep your existing folder structure ready for browsing.

Step 2

Generate Thumbnails

Create lightweight thumbnails for folders so large photo and video collections feel faster to open.

Step 3

Browse in the Viewer

Explore your media like a gallery with quick previews, smooth scrolling, and easier revisiting.

Privacy

Your private data stays on your iPhone

Personal information and app data are stored only as local data on your iPhone. TorchPhoto does not upload or share your private data anywhere.
